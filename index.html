LuckedIn Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

LuckedIn is a Chrome extension that fills in job application forms from a profile you create. This policy explains what it handles and where that data goes. In short: everything stays in your browser. LuckedIn has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads, and the developer never receives your data.

What LuckedIn stores, and where

All of it is kept in your browser's extension storage on your computer (chrome.storage), and nowhere else.

  • Your profile: name, contact details, address, links, work history, education, skills, work authorization, application preferences (such as expected salary and start date), and your settings.
  • Voluntary self-identification answers (optional): gender, race/ethnicity, veteran status, disability status, sexual orientation, transgender identity. Every one defaults to "Decline to self-identify"; you choose whether to change it.
  • Your resume file, to upload to applications you fill.
  • Saved answers (only if you turn on Remember answers I type on application pages): questions you answered by hand on job application pages, your answers, the site's name and the date.

What LuckedIn reads on web pages

  • LuckedIn runs by itself only on the websites of job application systems (such as Workday, Greenhouse, Lever and iCIMS). On any other website it does nothing unless you click its icon in Chrome's toolbar on that page, or choose Keep on for this site; you can take that back in LuckedIn's Settings or in Chrome's extension settings.
  • Where it runs, LuckedIn looks at the form fields and their labels to recognise a job application. This happens on your computer; nothing about the pages is stored or sent anywhere.
  • When you click Autofill, it types your profile data into that page's form. What you then submit to an employer is governed by the employer's privacy policy.
  • If you turn on Remember answers, it reads answers you type into job application pages only (not other websites), so it can offer them again.

What LuckedIn does not do

  • It does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else. It makes no network requests of its own.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • It does not track your browsing or keep any history of the sites you visit.
  • It does not submit applications on its own: it submits only when you click Submit (twice, to confirm), or when you turn on Handoff and start it, which goes through the steps and submits for you.
  • It does not load code from the internet; everything it runs is part of the extension package.

Troubleshooting files

If you click Save page for troubleshooting, LuckedIn saves a copy of the current page, including the answers on it, to your Downloads folder. It is never sent anywhere automatically; share it only if you choose to.

Your control

  • Review, change or delete any saved answer in the side panel or in LuckedIn home (Saved answers).
  • Export everything to a file, or Delete all my data in LuckedIn home (Settings).
  • Uninstalling LuckedIn removes all its stored data.
  • Chrome's Site access setting for the extension lets you limit which sites it can run on.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs and Chrome extension APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. LuckedIn uses your data only to provide its single purpose: filling in job applications for you.

Children

LuckedIn is meant for job seekers and is not directed to children under 16.

Changes

If LuckedIn's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the change shown in the extension before it takes effect.
